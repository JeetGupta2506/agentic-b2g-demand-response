Agentic AI HEMS Simulation

This system uses a ReAct-based orchestrator to coordinate specialist agents for optimal scheduling of residential flexible loads. The Agentic AI architecture leverages day-ahead electricity prices to minimize operational costs while respecting technical and comfort constraints.
