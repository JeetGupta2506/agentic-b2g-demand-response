We built on an open-source 2025 paper. In it, the language model itself chooses appliance start times. Five gaps: the LLM does arithmetic it is bad at; there is no solar or weather forecast; no battery or cooling, and nothing makes devices fit under a shared grid limit; nothing checks the result; and plans are judged on the same prices they were made with. The right column is our answer to each gap.
