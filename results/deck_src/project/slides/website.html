The product — step 1

Type what you need, in plain English

Plan my day

forecast · 3 agents · optimizer · verifier

plan in seconds

It understands things like

“leaving at 7 instead of 8”

“I need the car full”

“keep it under 26 °C”

“working from home all day”

“battery only from solar”

This is the product. The person types a normal sentence and picks the day. Pressing Plan my day runs the whole pipeline: forecast, three specialist agents, the optimizer and the verifier. A fresh request takes about 10 to 60 seconds on the free model; our live test took 12 seconds, and a repeated request is instant because answers are cached.
