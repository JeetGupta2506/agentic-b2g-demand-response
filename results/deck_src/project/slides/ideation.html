Ideation

Move the loads that can wait

Car charging

Only needs to be full by departure: charge slowly overnight.

Home battery

Fill it with free midday solar, use it in the evening peak.

Pre-cooling

A house warms slowly: cool it in the sunny afternoon.

What if the house planned its own day from one sentence?

“I’m leaving at 7 tomorrow, I need the car at 90%, keep the room under 26 °C.”

Some electricity use cannot move, like lights at night. Three big uses can: car charging, the home battery and cooling. The idea: let the person speak normally and let the AI plan the day around those three flexible loads.
