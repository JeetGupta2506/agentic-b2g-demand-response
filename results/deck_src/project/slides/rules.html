How it works — one request

From one sentence to exact rules

“Tomorrow I’m leaving at 7 instead of 8 and I need the car at 90%. I’m working from home from 2 pm, keep it under 26 °C. Keep at least 30% in the battery.”

StepWhat happens
Forecast + calendarTomorrow’s sun, heat and the usual routine
3 specialist agentsReturn only what changed, as strict JSON
OptimizerCar 00:00–07:00; battery filled from solar
VerifierPassed; 26.9 °C warning told honestly
Walk through the demo request for Wednesday 6 May 2026. The orchestrator reads the forecast and the usual routine, then asks the EV, battery and pre-cooling agents. Each returns only what changed: departure 07:00 and 90 percent, reserve 30 percent, max 26 degrees and home from 14:00. The optimizer plans the day. The verifier passes it with one warning: the AC is too small to hold 26 degrees at the hottest moment, so the person is told the room may reach 26.9.
