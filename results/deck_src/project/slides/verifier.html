The verifier at work

Caught: a setting nobody asked for

“I’m working from home all day tomorrow, please keep the house at most 25 degrees.”

  1. The pre-cooling agent also set a minimum of 22 °C on its own.
  2. The verifier rejected it: not supported by the request.
  3. The agent removed it; the second check passed.
  4. The person was told honestly: the room may reach 26.9 °C.

Four groups of checks: physics, the person’s wishes, the request itself, and every number in the final answer.

A real run, not staged. The language model invented a minimum temperature of 22 degrees. The verifier is plain code: it compares every rule with what the person actually said, rejected the invented value, and sent it back. The agent fixed it and the plan passed. This is why we never trust the LLM’s output without checking it.
