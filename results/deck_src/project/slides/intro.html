Introduction

Buildings can help the grid by choosing when they use power

Demand response

Use less electricity at the evening peak and more when power is plentiful.

Building-to-Grid (B2G)

The building uses its own solar, avoids peaks and stays under its grid limit.

Microgrid

A small local network with its own generation: a house, a campus, a village.
