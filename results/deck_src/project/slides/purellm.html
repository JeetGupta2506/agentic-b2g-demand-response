Results — the pure-LLM test

Why not let the LLM do everything?

Same request, same forecast, but the LLM writes the whole schedule itself.

₹3,633

total with penalties, vs ₹247 for our system

Its ₹85 bill looks cheap only because it did not do the job. Its own reasoning and its schedule disagreed.

We tested the alternative: give the LLM the same request and forecast and let it write the full schedule. It broke 51 physical rules, for example charging the car while the car was away, left the car 20 kWh short and let the room reach 30 degrees. Its reasoning even said charge from 04:00 to 07:00, but its schedule did something else. This is the strongest argument for our design.
